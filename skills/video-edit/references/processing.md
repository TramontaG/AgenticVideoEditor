# Processing decisions

Scene detection uses FFmpeg `select=gt(scene,threshold)` and `showinfo`; thresholds are heuristics. Combine cuts with periodic thumbnails and transcripts. Always inspect frames around ambiguous cuts. Silence detection uses `silencedetect`; music/noise may mask pauses. Keep speech margins, merge adjacent ranges, retain original source timestamps and regenerate subtitle timing after cuts.

`remove-bg` defaults to a small local image model. Video mode samples frames at the selected FPS, removes each background, writes ProRes 4444 with alpha and carries source audio if present. It is a baseline, not temporally trained matting. Preview hair, hands, motion blur and occlusions. Use a suitable temporal model for shots that show unstable edges; install that optional workflow only after checking hardware and upstream model requirements. For green screens use FFmpeg chromakey instead of inference where appropriate. Composite the result with a chosen image in Remotion, keeping the cutout separate for revisions. Do not call framewise masks temporally consistent without testing.

Normalization transcodes video to constant timeline FPS, H.264 yuv420p and audio to AAC, using two-pass loudnorm when audio exists. It preserves aspect ratio and does not apply creative crops. For audio-only sources it writes normalized AAC/M4A or WAV according to the destination. Target loudness defaults to -16 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP; choose delivery-specific targets when requested. Do not normalize every sound effect to speech loudness. Preserve originals and avoid cascading lossy transcodes.

For music ducking, first use the actual narration timing to construct a Remotion volume envelope with attack/release, or preprocess music with FFmpeg `sidechaincompress` driven by narration. Keep music, narration and effects separately editable. Listen across speech boundaries. Basic fades are supported by the template; arbitrary envelopes belong in project-local composition code. Stock music is subject to its own permissions.

Routine QA: verify dimensions/FPS/duration/audio streams, sample decoded frames, inspect scene and edit boundaries, and listen. `check` reports black and freeze candidates; intentional black holds and still graphics can trigger them. It cannot certify semantic correctness, safe framing or absence of flicker. Export a clear review artifact and disclose any unverified aspect.
